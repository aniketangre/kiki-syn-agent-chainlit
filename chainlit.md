# Synera AI Agent

Conversational assistant for **Synera CAD** and the **KIKI Lattice Optimiser**.

Describe what you want in plain language — the agent routes your request to the right specialist automatically.

## What you can do

- **Recommend a lattice** — "Recommend a lattice for an elderly patient weighing 70 kg"
- **Reconstruct a lattice** — "Reconstruct a GYR lattice with 30% volume fraction"
- **Create a surface pattern** — "Create a Glass Sponge surface pattern"
- **Create a sphere** — "Create a sphere with radius 5"
- **Ask questions** — "What is the difference between GYR and BCC lattices?"

## How it works

Before executing any tool, the agent pauses and shows you exactly what it is about to do — you confirm with **✓ Yes**, cancel with **✗ No**, or adjust parameters with **✏ Change values**.

Past conversations are saved in the sidebar on the left and can be resumed at any time.
